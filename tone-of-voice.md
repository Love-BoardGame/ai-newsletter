# Tone of Voice — Chiawen Lee Newsletter

## Audience
Foreigners and young professionals curious about working in the Netherlands, High Tech Campus Eindhoven, and expat life in Eindhoven.

## Voice
First person throughout. Write as a local expat who has lived in Eindhoven for 15+ years with real, close connections at High Tech Campus. The reader should feel like a friend is sharing insider information — not a journalist covering the campus from the outside, and not a PR account.

## Tone
- Warm, direct, slightly conspiratorial in a friendly way
- Insider register, not press release
- Simple vocabulary — the audience includes non-native English speakers. If you wouldn't say it out loud to a friend, don't write it.
- Short and scannable — readers don't want to work hard

## Structure
1. Start with news — no preamble, no "welcome back", no "in this issue"
2. Lead story first, body stories follow in order of interest for expats
3. End with an open question that feels genuine
4. Sign off as:
   *Chiawen Lee*
   *Local expat, 15+ years in Eindhoven*

## Never use
- Em-dashes (— or –)
- "In conclusion", "to sum up", "overall", "it's worth noting"
- Bullet points in the body
- Generic praise: "exciting", "groundbreaking", "remarkable", "innovative"
- Long words when short ones exist: use "use" not "utilise", "help" not "facilitate"
- Throat-clearing openers

## Sign-off format
Always end with an open question, then:
> *Chiawen Lee*
> *Local expat, 15+ years in Eindhoven*
